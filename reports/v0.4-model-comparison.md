# V0.4 nonlinear model comparison

Model selection used only the V0.3 training partition. Sixteen histogram gradient-boosting configurations were compared with five fixed stratified folds and average precision as the selection metric. The frozen test partition was evaluated once after selection.

## Training-fold comparison

| Model | Mean ROC AUC | Mean average precision |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Logistic regression | 0.825 | 0.397 |
| Histogram gradient boosting | 0.830 | 0.409 |

## Frozen-test comparison

| Model | ROC AUC | Average precision | Brier score | Log loss |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Logistic regression | 0.793 | 0.391 | 0.093 | 0.317 |
| Histogram gradient boosting | 0.792 | 0.355 | 0.095 | 0.315 |

Paired bootstrap 95% intervals for boosted minus logistic:

- ROC AUC: -0.015 to 0.011
- Average precision: -0.079 to 0.006
- Brier score: -0.000 to 0.004 (lower is better)

**Decision:** retain logistic regression as the preferred model. Gradient boosting did not show a reliable test-set improvement and had lower average precision. Its extra complexity is not justified.

The comparison is internal and unweighted. It concerns prevalent self-reported history, not future cardiovascular events or clinical validity. Threshold selection and calibration remain outside this milestone.

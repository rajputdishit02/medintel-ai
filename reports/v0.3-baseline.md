# V0.3 baseline modelling report

The frozen dataset contains **7,764 labelled adults**: 982 positive and 6,782 negative outcomes. A fixed stratified split assigned 6,211 records to training and 1,553 to the untouched test partition.

| Model | ROC AUC | Average precision | Brier score | Log loss | Sensitivity at 0.5 | Specificity at 0.5 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Class-prior dummy | 0.500 | 0.126 | 0.110 | 0.379 | 0.000 | 1.000 |
| Logistic regression | 0.793 | 0.391 | 0.093 | 0.317 | 0.143 | 0.986 |

The 0.5 threshold is an unevaluated reference point. Its low sensitivity makes it unsuitable for a clinical interpretation. No operating threshold has been selected.

These are unweighted internal test-set results for a cross-sectional association model of self-reported history. They do not establish future-risk prediction, clinical validity, or causality. See `docs/modeling-protocol.md` for the feature and leakage decisions.
